# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Kanyah serves children reading short African stories on mobile devices, alongside parents who create and manage child profiles. Children need a focused, understandable reading experience with controls that work well for small hands and developing readers. Parents need clear separation from child-facing areas and straightforward profile controls.

## Product Purpose

Kanyah brings African storytelling to mobile screens through illustrated, readable, and optionally narrated stories. Success means a child can choose a story, read or listen without adult assistance, resume their place, and feel encouraged to continue.

## Brand Personality

Vibrant, warm, and imaginative. The experience should feel rooted in African visual storytelling while remaining calm enough for sustained reading.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense adult dashboards, generic educational software, excessive controls, decorative motion, and interfaces that hide essential actions behind unfamiliar gestures. Do not let responsive web framing make the child experience feel like a desktop application.

## Design Principles

- Make the child’s next action obvious.
- Keep reading content visually dominant.
- Reveal secondary controls only when needed.
- Use familiar controls and forgiving touch targets.
- Preserve a consistent African visual identity without sacrificing readability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable type, sufficient contrast, large touch targets, explicit accessibility labels, and reduced-motion-safe transitions. Never rely on color alone to communicate playback or progress state. Narration is optional enhancement, and every story must remain usable as text.
